# Forwarding all your Gmail to another address (for testing)

This guide shows how to send a copy of every new email that arrives in your Gmail to another email address, for
example a test mailbox. Your Gmail keeps working as usual; you can switch the forwarding off again at any time.

It takes about 5 minutes. You need a **computer** with a web browser: the Gmail app on a phone can't set this up.

---

## Before you start

- **The other address must be able to receive email**, and you must be able to open its inbox: Gmail sends a
  confirmation message there.
- **Two-step verification (2FA) is not required** for forwarding. Google may still ask you to **prove it's you**
  while you set it up (see step 4): it asks for your password again, or sends a "Is it you?" prompt to your phone.
  If you have 2-step verification switched on, have your phone ready.
- **Work or school accounts** (an address that doesn't end in @gmail.com, run by your company or school): your
  administrator may have switched forwarding off. If the option is missing or greyed out, ask them.

---

## Step by step

1. **Open Gmail** in your browser at [mail.google.com](https://mail.google.com) and sign in.

2. **Open the settings.** Click the **gear** icon at the top right, then **See all settings**.

3. **Go to the forwarding tab.** In the row of tabs at the top, click **Forwarding and POP/IMAP**.

4. **Add the address.** Click **Add a forwarding address**, type the other email address and click **Next**, then
   **Proceed**, then **OK**.
   If Google asks you to sign in again or to confirm on your phone, do so: this is the "prove it's you" check.

5. **Confirm from the other mailbox.** Gmail now sends a message titled something like *"Gmail Forwarding
   Confirmation"* to the other address. Open that mailbox, open the message and click the confirmation link.
   (It also contains a confirmation code; you can type it into the Gmail settings page instead.)
   Nothing is forwarded until this is done. Can't find it? Look in the spam folder.

6. **Switch forwarding on.** Go back to the Gmail settings tab and reload the page (the same **Forwarding and
   POP/IMAP** tab). Choose **Forward a copy of incoming mail to** and pick the address you just confirmed.

7. **Choose what happens to the original** in Gmail. For testing, pick **keep Gmail's copy in the Inbox**: your
   Gmail then stays exactly as before.

8. Scroll to the bottom and click **Save Changes**.

Done. Send yourself a test email: it should arrive in Gmail and, a moment later, in the other mailbox.

---

## Good to know

- **Only new email is forwarded**, from the moment you save. Email already in your Gmail stays where it is.
- **Spam is not forwarded.**
- For about a week, Gmail shows a reminder at the top of your inbox that forwarding is on. That's normal.
- The forwarded emails arrive looking almost exactly like the originals: same sender, subject and content.

---

## Switching it off again

Gear icon → **See all settings** → **Forwarding and POP/IMAP** → choose **Disable forwarding** → **Save Changes**.

To also remove the address completely, open the drop-down next to *Forward a copy of incoming mail to* and choose
**Remove** followed by the address.
